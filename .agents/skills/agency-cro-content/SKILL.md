# Agency CRO & Content Strategy Skill

This skill guides the messaging architecture, conversion rate optimization (CRO), search-intent content strategy, and trustworthy proof hierarchy for Qubizon Digital Solutions (*"All your digital needs in one cube"*).

## Core Principles & Constraints

1. **Clear Brand Positioning**: Every page must communicate Qubizon's value proposition within 5 seconds: unifying fragmented digital services (Web, SEO, Social, Ads, Content) under one disciplined, high-velocity agency partner.
2. **Absolute Fact & Proof Hygiene**:
   - Never fabricate client logos, fake testimonial quotes, false case study metrics (e.g. "+840% ROI in 2 weeks"), or unverified industry awards.
   - For early-stage or demo states, use authentic capability demonstrations, transparent methodology breakdowns, live sample audits, or clearly labeled demo scenarios instead of fabricated claims.
3. **No Thin or Manipulative SEO**:
   - Zero tolerance for keyword stuffing, door-way pages, or low-value auto-generated thin content.
   - Every service page must deliver comprehensive, expert-level utility answering real search intent.
4. **Frictionless, Accessible Conversions**:
   - Every call to action (CTA) must set clear expectations (e.g., "Request a Technical SEO Audit" vs vague "Submit").
   - Lead forms must be accessible, minimal, and respect user privacy.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

### Step 1: Homepage & Value Proposition Architecture
- **Hero Section**:
  - Primary headline: Punchy, outcome-focused statement centered around the unified "cube" concept.
  - Subheadline: Clear explanation of what Qubizon does, for whom, and why the unified model out-executes fragmented vendors.
  - Dual CTAs: Primary CTA for immediate high-intent prospects (e.g., "Schedule Strategy Session" or "Get a Website & SEO Audit") + Secondary CTA for exploration (e.g., "Explore Our Services Cube").
- **Unified Service Matrix (The Cube)**:
  - Present the 5 core disciplines:
    1. **Website Development**: High-performance, accessible, custom-engineered digital platforms.
    2. **Search Engine Optimization**: Technical SEO, content authority, and organic pipeline growth.
    3. **Social Media Marketing**: Brand storytelling, community engagement, and social distribution.
    4. **Paid Advertising**: Data-backed PPC, paid social, and conversion-optimized ad campaigns.
    5. **Content Marketing**: Search-aligned editorial, thought leadership, and high-value lead magnets.
- **Process & Methodology**:
  - Transparent 3-to-4 step execution framework (Discovery & Audit → Strategy & Architecture → High-Velocity Execution → Continuous Optimization).

### Step 2: Search-Intent Mapping & Topical Authority Architecture
- Structure topical silos with clear parent-child URL hierarchies:
  - `/services/web-development`
  - `/services/seo`
  - `/services/social-media-marketing`
  - `/services/paid-advertising`
  - `/services/content-marketing`
- For each service page:
  - Target specific high-intent transactional and commercial search queries.
  - Detail specific deliverables, tech stacks, workflow milestones, and FAQs.
  - Include relevant internal links connecting complementary services (e.g., linking SEO service pages to Web Development architecture).

### Step 3: Conversion Funnel & Lead Capture Design
- **Interactive Scope / Service Selector**:
  - Allow visitors to toggle or select their required service combination from the "Cube", generating an instant tailored consultation outline.
- **Form Design & Usability**:
  - Keep initial inquiries low-friction: Name, Business Email, Website URL, Primary Goal.
  - Provide inline client-side validation for instant UX feedback, paired with strict server-side validation.
  - Implement accessible labels (`<label for="...">`), clear error announcements (`aria-describedby`, `aria-live="polite"`), and clear success confirmations.
- **Micro-Conversion Pathways**:
  - Offer lower-commitment conversion options for exploratory prospects (e.g., free automated website audit checklist or strategy guide).

### Step 4: Verification & Trust Validation
- Ensure all trust assets on the site (case studies, process diagrams, security badges, performance benchmarks) are verified.
- Present quantifiable business value through transparent case studies:
  - The Client Challenge & Context.
  - The Technical & Strategic Solution.
  - Verifiable Outcomes & Key Takeaways.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Headline and value proposition explicitly communicate the "All your digital needs in one cube" model.
- [ ] The 5 core digital disciplines clearly defined with distinct value propositions.
- [ ] No placeholder or fake client testimonials, metrics, logos, or awards present in code.
- [ ] Service pages structured with logical URL hierarchy and contextual internal links.
- [ ] Lead forms follow WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility guidelines with visible labels and accessible error states.
- [ ] Primary and secondary CTAs use action-oriented, transparent language.
